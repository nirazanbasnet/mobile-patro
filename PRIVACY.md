# Privacy Policy for MobilePatro

**Last updated: 19 July 2026**

MobilePatro ("the app") is a Nepali (Bikram Sambat) calendar app. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The app has no accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.

## What stays on your device

The following are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us, and we cannot see them:

- **Your app settings** — language, theme, and display preferences
- **Notes** you attach to dates
- **Custom holidays** you create
- **Events** you add, including their titles, notes, dates, and reminder times

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data through your device settings.

## What leaves your device

The app connects to only two services.

### 1. Calendar data

To display calendar details, the app downloads public Nepali calendar data from `data.miti.bikram.io`. These requests contain no personal information. As with any internet request, the operator of that service can see your device's IP address.

### 2. AI features (only when you choose to use them)

Two optional features send data to our server, which forwards the request to Cerebras, an AI provider, and returns the result. **These requests are made only when you actively use one of these features.** If you never use them, nothing is ever sent.

- **Ritual Guide** — sends the name of the selected *tithi* (lunar day) and your chosen language. It does not send your notes, events, or any personal information.
- **Smart Add** — sends **the text you type into it**, along with the current Nepali date, so that it can be interpreted into an event. If you type personal details into this field, that text is transmitted for processing.

Requests are processed by Cerebras under [Cerebras's privacy policy](https://www.cerebras.ai/privacy-policy). Cerebras states that it [does not retain](https://support.cerebras.net/articles/1811589793-does-cerebras-retain-my-data) prompt content or API requests and responses. We do not use this data to build a profile of you, and we do not link it to any identity, because the app holds no identity for you.

Your saved notes, events, and holidays are **never** sent to the AI service in the background or in bulk. Only the specific text you enter at the moment you use a feature is transmitted.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app asks permission to send notifications. All reminders are scheduled and delivered **locally by your own device**. The app does not register for push notifications and does not have a push token or a notification server. Reminder content is never transmitted anywhere.

You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
- We do **not** collect your location. The app does not request location access.
- We do **not** access your camera, photos, contacts, or files.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with anyone for marketing.
- We do **not** create user profiles or track you across apps or websites.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose calendar and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

Because your content is stored only on your device, you control it directly:

- Delete individual notes, events, or custom holidays inside the app.
- Clear all app data through your device's app settings.
- Uninstall the app to remove everything.
- Simply not use the Ritual Guide or Smart Add features, if you prefer that nothing be transmitted for AI processing.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
